# 🔑 Banking Officer Login & Password Reset

This user guide provides step-by-step instructions for banking officers to securely sign into the Janata CBS Core Banking Workbench, manage session authentications, and perform mandatory or routine password resets.

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose

The **Janata CBS Workbench** is the official core banking portal used by branch officers, cash tellers, branch managers, and system administrators to process daily banking operations. Secure authentication ensures that all financial transactions, account inquiries, and system activities are properly authorized and audited.

---

## 2. Who Can Use This Feature

- **Authorized Branch Officers** (Tellers, Relationship Managers, Loan Officers)
- **Branch Operation Managers & Supervisors**
- **System Administrators**

---

## 3. Prerequisites

Before logging in, ensure you have:
1. An active, approved **User ID** issued by your IT Administrator.
2. Your initial or current **Account Password**.
3. Access to an authorized banking terminal connected to the Janata Bank network.
4. Your designated **Branch Code** (e.g., `0014 - Main Branch`).

> [!CAUTION]
> **Security Notice:** Never share your User ID, Password, or Authentication PIN with anyone. Bank personnel will never ask for your credentials.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Login Procedure

Follow these exact steps to initiate your daily banking session:

1. Open your browser and navigate to the **Janata CBS Workbench** web URL.
2. In the **User ID** field, enter your assigned corporate officer identifier (e.g., `OFFICER01`).
3. In the **Password** field, enter your current secure password.
4. Select your assigned **Branch Location / Branch Code** from the dropdown selector.
5. Click **Sign In**.

### Expected Result
Upon successful verification, the system loads the **Workbench Main Dashboard**, displaying your active business date, assigned branch, and navigation sidebar menu.

---

## 5. Mandatory First-Time & Expired Password Reset

If you are logging in with a temporary password or if your password has expired (every 90 days), the system will automatically prompt you to update your credentials.

### Step-by-Step Reset Procedure:

1. When prompted by the **Mandatory Password Reset** screen:
   - Enter your **Current Password** (or temporary password).
   - Enter your **New Password**.
   - Re-enter your **New Password** in the **Confirm New Password** field.
2. Click **Update Password**.

### Password Format & Strength Rules:
- Minimum length of **8 characters**.
- Must contain at least **one uppercase letter (A–Z)**.
- Must contain at least **one lowercase letter (a–z)**.
- Must contain at least **one numeric digit (0–9)**.
- Must contain at least **one special character** (e.g., `@`, `#`, `$`, `!`).
- Cannot match your previous 3 passwords.

---

## 6. Common Errors & Troubleshooting

| Error Message | Cause | Corrective Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Invalid User ID or Password** | Incorrect credentials entered. | Verify caps lock is off and re-enter credentials carefully. |
| **Account Locked (3 Failed Attempts)** | Account suspended due to multiple invalid password attempts. | Contact your Branch System Administrator to unlock your profile. |
| **Session Expired Due to Inactivity** | Terminal left idle for over 15 minutes. | Click **Re-Authenticate**, re-enter your password, and resume work. |
| **Unauthorized Branch Selected** | Selected branch code does not match your assigned branch profile. | Select your assigned home branch code from the dropdown. |

---

## 7. Logging Out Safely

To ensure security when leaving your workstation:
1. Click your **User Profile Avatar** in the top-right toolbar.
2. Select **Sign Out Session**.
3. Confirm the logout prompt when asked: *"Are you sure you want to terminate your active session?"*
