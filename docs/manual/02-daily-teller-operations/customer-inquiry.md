# 🔍 Customer & Account Inquiries Guide

This user guide provides operational instructions for branch officers to perform customer profile lookups, view account ledger balances, inspect historical transaction records, and export statement reports.

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose

The **Inquiry Engine** provides real-time read-only visibility into customer profiles, account balances, lien holds, loan schedules, and past transaction histories. It allows officers to handle customer service requests efficiently while enforcing data privacy policies.

---

## 2. Who Can Use This Feature

- **Customer Service Representatives & Tellers**
- **Branch Relationship Managers**
- **Audit & Compliance Officers**

---

## 3. Searching Customer Profiles & Accounts

1. Open the left sidebar menu and navigate to **Inquiries → Customer Search** (or press **`Ctrl + K`** and search for `Customer Inquiry`).
2. Select your lookup criteria:
   - **Customer ID / CIF** (e.g., `CIF-102938`)
   - **Account Number** (e.g., `0014-01029384`)
   - **National ID / Passport Number**
   - **Customer Full Name**
3. Click **Search**.
4. The system populates matching records in the interactive data grid.

---

## 4. Single-Item Detailed Record View

To inspect the full details of a specific account or customer profile:

1. Click on any row entry in the search results data grid.
2. The view shifts to the **Single Item Record** screen.
3. Review specific account panels:
   - **General Information:** Account Title, Opening Date, Account Type, Account Status.
   - **Balance Summary:** Ledger Balance, Available Balance, Uncleared Balance, Lien Amount.
   - **Account Signature & Photo:** Verified customer specimen signature card for identity confirmation.
   - **Recent Transactions:** Itemized list of recent debit/credit entries with reference voucher IDs.

---

## 5. Exporting & Printing Inquiry Reports

To provide account statements or audit printouts to customers:

1. On the Inquiry screen, click the **Export / Print** button located on the top right toolbar.
2. Select your desired format:
   - **Download PDF Statement** (Official bank layout with header/footer)
   - **Print Preview** (Send directly to branch receipt printer)
3. Confirm the export prompt.

---

## 6. Common Inquiry Warnings & Error Messages

| Warning / Message | Meaning | Corrective Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **No Records Found** | Search criteria returned zero matching profiles. | Double-check Account Number formatting or Customer CIF ID. |
| **Restricted Access Record** | Confidential profile (e.g., VIP or Staff Account). | Request supervisor elevation or permission override. |
| **Signature Specimen Missing** | Customer signature card not uploaded. | Direct customer to Customer Service desk for specimen scan update. |
